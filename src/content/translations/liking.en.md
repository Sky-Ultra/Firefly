Sometimes I feel

that it's on ordinary days

that we slowly become who we are now.

Nothing particularly special happens,

but the people around us keep changing.

We meet new people,

and drift apart from others.

We've listened to the songs we love for years,

and only when we look back do we realize

how much time has passed.

I used to love summer,

leaving the window open at night,

watching the wind move the curtains back and forth.

I'd listen to music with my headphones on,

or lie in bed reading a book.

When I got sleepy,

I'd lay the book over my face and sleep for a while.

I still remember

one night when I watched the moon for a long time.

It was an ordinary summer night,

and nothing worth remembering had happened,

but I still told it about my dreams.

I told the moon my dreams.

I'm selfish,

and I hope it remembers them forever.

Back then,

I still believed in things that felt far away,

and hoped someone would come into my life,

thinking that if I kept caring,

something would surely remain.

But that's not how it works,

not every affection leaves something behind.

Once some people leave,

they really never come back.

Some words are left unfinished,

and no one will wait forever to hear the rest.

Later,

there was a long time when I couldn't sleep.

Thinking about someone, about something they'd said,

about the things that never worked out.

Later still,

the nights grew quieter and quieter.

No one was waiting for me,

and there was nothing I absolutely had to figure out.

I don't have insomnia anymore.

I go to bed early,

a good night's sleep matters more than love.

There are no new people,

and I haven't contacted anyone from before.

People say I'm cold.

It's not my fault,

you can't keep standing in the cold wind forever.

I used to think

that if someone really cared,

they should hold on a little longer.

Now that I think about it,

they don't have to.

No one disappears,

people only stay in touch with those they miss.

I find it harder and harder to say what love is.

Maybe I cared too much before,

always feeling I couldn't afford to lose anything.

Now there aren't so many things I "absolutely must have."

I don't have any particular wishes this autumn,

I just hope that the next time I see a moon I love,

I'll still remember to look up at it.

As for the dreams that never came true…

Let them be,

it's enough that the moon remembers them for me.

The moonlight really is beautiful tonight,

but I'll still go to bed early.

A good night's sleep matters more than love.
