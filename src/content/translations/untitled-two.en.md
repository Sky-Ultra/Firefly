Ambiguity is as cheap as junk, infatuation is as deadly as poison.

暧昧和破烂一样廉价，痴情和毒药一样致命。

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

Love can be pretended, but secret love cannot.

爱是可以装出来的，但暗恋不会。

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

It is love that cannot be restrained, and love is not sober.

克制不住的才叫爱，爱本就是不清醒的。

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

Falling in love is the guidance of fate, something predestined.

相爱是宿命的指引，是命中注定。

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

我们看见的星星早已远去，但它们的光依然指引着我们，正如真正的连接一般。

The stars we see are long gone, yet their light still guides us, much like a true connection.

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

我们并非不完整；另一个人不是我们的“缺失部分”，而是折射我们灵魂的镜子。

We are not incomplete; another is not our 'missing piece', but rather a mirror that reflects our own soul.

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

最美丽的花朵往往有最深的根，需要时间来滋养，而不只是阳光。

The most beautiful flowers often have the deepest roots, nurtured over time, not just sunlight.
