Summer has come again.

To be honest, I do not like summer very much.

The air is hot, and so is the wind.

It feels stifling outside, and inside me too,

as though something were weighing on me.

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

The cicadas keep calling,

from morning until noon,

from noon until evening.

Sometimes I think they are shouting too,

shouting things nobody can understand.

I lie in bed,

listening to one cicada call after another outside the window,

and suddenly feel irritated.

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

<mark class="theme-highlight">After growing up, I often feel anxious for no apparent reason.</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight">I used to think nineteen was so far away,</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight">then discovered that nineteen was nothing so remarkable after all.</mark>

I used to think there would be plenty of time ahead,

then discovered that the time ahead kept getting shorter.

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

Sometimes I wake in the middle of the night,

stare at the ceiling, and think about so many things.

Work, the future, money,

and things that have not even happened.

By the time I have thought it all through, there are no answers,

only the approaching dawn,

and the cicadas about to start calling again.

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

<mark class="theme-highlight">I always feel that people these days are in such a hurry.</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight">In a hurry to walk, in a hurry to speak,</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight">even in a hurry to fall for someone.</mark>

After three days of knowing someone, they say they miss them;

after a week, they speak of forever;

after a month, they have already begun saying goodbye.

As though someone had pressed fast-forward on everything.

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

<mark class="theme-highlight">I used to think that liking someone was a slow thing.</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight">It was walking together for a long time on the way home from school,</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight">being unable to bring yourself to delete the chat history,</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight">remembering what someone liked to drink.</mark>

It does not seem that way anymore.

Now everyone is busy meeting people, busy leaving,

busy starting the next story,

yet nobody has time to stop.

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

The phone keeps ringing,

the messages keep refreshing,

the short videos keep scrolling past.

Watch one, then another, then another.

Before I have had time to remember much of anything,

something new has already covered it up.

Sometimes I scroll for a long time,

<mark class="theme-highlight">but once I put down my phone, I cannot remember a thing.</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight">I only remember that time has passed,</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight">and the cicadas are still calling outside.</mark>

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

Summer is always like this.

The sun is strong, the sky is bright,

yet somehow it is easy to feel low.

The trees along the road grow thick with leaves,

their shadows falling across the pavement.

<mark class="theme-highlight">Some people pass by, some people leave,</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight">and nobody stops.</mark>

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

Sometimes I stare at a leaf for a long time,

watching the wind blow it back and forth,

and suddenly feel that leaves have an easier time than people.

At least a leaf does not have to think about the future,

or next year,

or whether it will become who it wants to be.

The cicadas are still calling. They really are noisy.

But if one day they suddenly stopped,

I think I would feel that something was missing.

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

People are always like this,

disliking certain things,

yet unable to let certain things go.

<mark class="theme-highlight">Like growing up, like saying goodbye,</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight">like those feelings that never lead anywhere.</mark>

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

It rained a few days ago.

The rain was heavy, and the ground was soaking wet.

The cicadas suddenly fell silent,

and the whole world fell silent too.

I stood by the window watching the rain,

thinking of nothing.

For once, thinking of nothing at all.

For those few minutes, the future did not arrive.

There was only rain, only wind,

only trees swaying wildly in the gusts.

Then the rain stopped,

the cicadas started calling again, the phone started ringing again,

and life continued moving forward.

As though nothing had happened.

In truth, nothing really had happened.

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

It was just an ordinary summer,

so ordinary that it resembled last year,

and the year before that too.

Yet I still remembered it.

I remembered a certain stifling afternoon,

a certain sleepless night,

a certain chorus of cicadas that would not stop.

<mark class="theme-highlight">I remembered the worries I could not put into words,</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight">the anxieties nobody knew about,</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight">the sadness that came without warning.</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight">Summer is long, and so is the future.</mark>

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

The cicadas do not know why they call,

and I do not know where I will end up.

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

Then let things be this way for now.

Let them keep calling,

let me keep walking.

After all, night will always fall,

rain will always come,

and summer will always pass.

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

<mark class="theme-highlight">And we all have to grow up, little by little.</mark>
