My heart can knock, tremble and come to a stop—am I a clock too?

My arms can stretch, support and fall—am I a bridge too?

My veins can coil, draw nourishment and extend—am I a root too?

The lines of my palms can crisscross, deepen and run dry—am I a river too?

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

My past can rise, drift and settle—am I dust too?

My fixation can cling, spread and wither—am I moss too?

My pain can grow, peel away and fall silent—am I rust too?

My forgiveness can cover, remain silent and melt—am I snow too?

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

My silence can deepen, swallow and echo—am I an abyss too?

My uncertainty can spread, shroud and disperse—am I mist too?

My companionship can lengthen, follow and disappear—am I a shadow too?

My half-lived life can toss, run aground and sail far away—am I a boat too?

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

My hair can grow, turn yellow and fall—am I a tree too?

My soul can float, wander and dissipate—am I a cloud too?

My thoughts can travel far, without color or form—am I the wind too?

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

My desires can erupt, diffuse and diffract—am I the rosy clouds too?

My anger can burn, rise and die out—am I fire too?

My will can tower, stand firm and crumble—am I a mountain too?

My thinking can grow rigid, cold and melt away—am I ice too?

My hands can clasp, entangle and break free—am I a lock too?

My eyes can refract light, cloud over and lose their color—am I the moon too?

My body can grow, embrace and decay—am I the earth too?

We can all love and hate, grow and travel far—surely that makes us human……

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>
