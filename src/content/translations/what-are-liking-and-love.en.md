Daylight saving time. It's past four in the morning, and the cicadas are still calling outside.

They've been like this ever since summer began in Sydney.

Calling through the day, and through the night,

as if they never get tired.

But I'm already getting a little fed up.

The room is stuffy. The air conditioner is on,

yet the hot air still clings to my skin.

I toss and turn.

When one side of the pillow gets warm, I turn it over.

And so it goes: warm, turn it over; turn it over, warm again.

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

Tell me, why is it that at night

we can't help overthinking?

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

In the daytime, I can manage not to think about anything.

Keep busy, get a little tired, walk a little faster,

and it seems I've forgotten it all.

But the moment things grow quiet,

everything I've kept hidden comes out on its own.

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

Things like liking someone. Things like love.

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

<mark class="theme-highlight" style="background-color: color-mix(in oklab, var(--selection-bg) 75%, transparent)">I always thought liking someone was simple.</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight" style="background-color: color-mix(in oklab, var(--selection-bg) 75%, transparent)">Liking someone means wanting to see her,</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight" style="background-color: color-mix(in oklab, var(--selection-bg) 75%, transparent)">wanting to tell her right away about something interesting I've seen,</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight" style="background-color: color-mix(in oklab, var(--selection-bg) 75%, transparent)">wanting to know whether she's had a good day.</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight" style="background-color: color-mix(in oklab, var(--selection-bg) 75%, transparent)">When she's sad, just be there for her.</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight" style="background-color: color-mix(in oklab, var(--selection-bg) 75%, transparent)">It really does sound simple.</mark>

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

But later I found it wasn't like that.

<mark class="theme-highlight" style="background-color: color-mix(in oklab, var(--selection-bg) 75%, transparent)">Once I like someone,</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight" style="background-color: color-mix(in oklab, var(--selection-bg) 75%, transparent)">I suddenly become so careful.</mark>

I turn a sentence over in my mind several times,

and think for ages before doing anything.

Afraid that messaging too often will annoy her;

afraid that getting close too quickly will disrupt her rhythm;

and afraid that what I think of as gentleness

might be something she never needed.

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

So sometimes I find myself wondering:

<mark class="theme-highlight" style="background-color: color-mix(in oklab, var(--selection-bg) 75%, transparent)">Do I really like her,</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight" style="background-color: color-mix(in oklab, var(--selection-bg) 75%, transparent)">or do I like the version of her I've imagined for myself?</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight" style="background-color: color-mix(in oklab, var(--selection-bg) 75%, transparent)">What is love, anyway?</mark>

Is it saying good morning and good night every day?

Is it checking in about everything I do?

Or is it being willing, for her sake,

to change the habits I've picked up from being alone for so long?

I seem to understand a little,

and yet I seem to understand nothing at all.

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

I've read so many stories about love.

Some people cross mountains and valleys.

Some give up so much.

Some say love means companionship;

others say it means letting the other person find happiness.

But when it comes to me,

I suddenly don't know how to do any of it.

Knowing the answer and actually doing it

turn out to be two different things.

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

There is someone I really like.

I want to give her all my special affection.

In this enormous world,

I want there to be someone special

to share meals and walks with,

to talk about all kinds of meaningless things with.

On an entirely ordinary day,

just seeing her smile

would make me feel that today wasn't bad after all.

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

But I'm afraid too.

Afraid I'm not good enough,

afraid that someone who can't even explain what love is

will end up letting her down.

Afraid that my presence

will only become a problem in her life.

So much of the time,

I want to take a step forward,

but take a step back instead.

I want to hold her,

but pull my hands back.

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

I rather hate growing up.

Once you're grown, even liking someone becomes so complicated.

As a child, when I liked a sweet, I had to eat it at once,

afraid someone else would take it if I waited.

If I liked a toy,

I would throw a tantrum to make it mine.

<mark class="theme-highlight" style="background-color: color-mix(in oklab, var(--selection-bg) 75%, transparent)">But now, when I like someone,</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight" style="background-color: color-mix(in oklab, var(--selection-bg) 75%, transparent)">I've learned restraint,</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight" style="background-color: color-mix(in oklab, var(--selection-bg) 75%, transparent)">learned to hide it,</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight" style="background-color: color-mix(in oklab, var(--selection-bg) 75%, transparent)">learned to think about so very much.</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight" style="background-color: color-mix(in oklab, var(--selection-bg) 75%, transparent)">Does that count as maturity,</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight" style="background-color: color-mix(in oklab, var(--selection-bg) 75%, transparent)">or is it only cowardice?</mark>

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

The cicadas are still calling outside.

They never seem to question

whether their voices mean anything.

They just call, and keep calling.

<mark class="theme-highlight" style="background-color: color-mix(in oklab, var(--selection-bg) 75%, transparent)">Suddenly, I envy them a little.</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight" style="background-color: color-mix(in oklab, var(--selection-bg) 75%, transparent)">Liking is simply liking; missing someone is simply missing them.</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight" style="background-color: color-mix(in oklab, var(--selection-bg) 75%, transparent)">No analysis, no proof,</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight" style="background-color: color-mix(in oklab, var(--selection-bg) 75%, transparent)">and no worrying about what comes later.</mark>

And people?

Our hearts are piled with so much:

the past, the future, expectations, fears, regrets,

and so many words we cannot say.

Sometimes I think I'm rather strange.

I long so much for someone to enter my world,

yet I'm afraid of being truly seen.

I want to love, but don't know how.

So I watch other people hold each other,

watch them say goodbye,

watch them begin new stories,

while I remain where I am,

never taking a step forward.

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

<mark class="theme-highlight" style="background-color: color-mix(in oklab, var(--selection-bg) 75%, transparent)">I really have met someone I want to cherish…</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight" style="background-color: color-mix(in oklab, var(--selection-bg) 75%, transparent)">What should I do?</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight" style="background-color: color-mix(in oklab, var(--selection-bg) 75%, transparent)">Should I be a little braver?</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight" style="background-color: color-mix(in oklab, var(--selection-bg) 75%, transparent)">Or keep my distance?</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight" style="background-color: color-mix(in oklab, var(--selection-bg) 75%, transparent)">Should I tell her I like her?</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight" style="background-color: color-mix(in oklab, var(--selection-bg) 75%, transparent)">Or wait until I'm good enough?</mark>

<mark class="theme-highlight" style="background-color: color-mix(in oklab, var(--selection-bg) 75%, transparent)">But when is anyone ever truly ready?</mark>

Life seems to have always been

moving forward while still afraid.

Right now,

I'm only lying here late on a summer night,

listening to cicadas, feeling a breeze that isn't really cool,

thinking about questions with no answers.

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

What does it mean to like someone?

What does it mean to love?

<div class="word-paragraph-gap" data-empty-paragraphs="1" style="--empty-paragraphs: 1" aria-hidden="true"></div>

And

how can I avoid letting down someone willing to come closer to me?
